# 1.8.1 verification

- An A plush (Seth Lowell) appeared in a purple character focus before the ten-pull reward grid. The diamond item in the same batch went directly to the grid.
- A guaranteed single S pull locked at eight seconds, then displayed Cissia in a gold character focus before her reward card.
- Agent voice playback is limited to the focus phase and starts 12 ticks after entry, when the character is visible. Leaving the focus stops that voice.
- The music transition was revised after the in-game visual checks. Entering an A or S focus no longer starts or stops the background track; it continues from the current playback position. The final reward grid stops the track. This last audio behavior was checked in code and build, not measured through speaker output.
- The development save's level and player data were restored from the pre-test backup after the game closed.

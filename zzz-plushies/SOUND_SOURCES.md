# Gacha audio sources

- `gacha_spin.ogg`: existing user-supplied Golden Sign recording, retained from 1.5.0.
- `gacha_ab.ogg`: full user-supplied `OST【ZENLESS ZONE ZERO】Gacha Theme (Instrumental) - Marion.mp3`, converted to streaming Vorbis for version 1.8.0. Duration: approximately 221.36 seconds; playback begins at the start of the recording. The B/A channel animation locks at 8 seconds, aligned with the musical drop, and the audio keeps playing until the player confirms.
- `gacha_vocal.ogg`: archived vocal-section edit from earlier versions. The current focus keeps the original `gacha_spin.ogg` playing continuously instead of restarting from this edit.
- `channel_static.ogg`: synthetic short TV tuning noise introduced in 1.6.0.
- `gacha_reveal.ogg`: original 0.9-second synthesized noise swoosh, bass impact and chime for the slide/focus transition.
- Agent dialogue sources and missing-voice coverage remain documented in `VOICE_SOURCES.md`.

The former 1.7.0 A/B audio is preserved in `versions/1.7.0/`.
